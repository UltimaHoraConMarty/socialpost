Privacy Policy

Effective October 19, 2026

socialpost (“the app”) is a personal publishing tool operated by Última Hora con Marty. It runs on the owner's own computer. This policy explains what data the app accesses, where it is kept, and how to remove it.

In short: the app only accesses the accounts you connect, only to publish your own videos. Everything it stores stays on your computer. We do not run a server, sell data, or share it with anyone other than the platform you are posting to.

Data the app accesses

  • Account basics from each platform you connect, used to show which account you are posting to:
    • YouTube: your channel name.
    • Facebook and Instagram: your Page's name and ID, and your linked Instagram account's username and ID.
    • TikTok: your open ID and display name (scope user.info.basic), and the posting options TikTok reports for your account, such as allowed privacy levels.
  • Access tokens that the platforms issue when you sign in, so the app can post on your behalf.
  • The videos, captions and thumbnails you choose to publish.

The app does not read your followers, messages, comments, contacts, or other people's content.

How the data is used

Only to upload and publish the content you select to the accounts you select, at the time you choose, and to show you the result. With TikTok, the app uses the scopes video.upload and video.publish solely to send your chosen video to your own TikTok account.

Where it is stored

  • Access tokens are stored on your computer in the macOS Keychain (or an encrypted local file).
  • A local history of posts (platform, file name, caption, link and status) and copies of videos queued for scheduled posting are kept in the app's folder on your computer.
  • Nothing is sent to the app's operator. Data is transmitted only directly to the platform you are posting to, over HTTPS.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share your data. Your content is sent only to the platform you choose to publish on, where that platform's own privacy policy applies:

  • Google Privacy Policy (YouTube). The app uses YouTube API Services; see also the YouTube Terms of Service.
  • Meta Privacy Policy (Facebook and Instagram)
  • TikTok Privacy Policy

Retention and deletion

  • Tokens are kept until you sign out of a platform in the app or revoke access.
  • To revoke access at any time: Google at myaccount.google.com/permissions; Facebook and Instagram under Settings → Business integrations; TikTok under Settings and privacy → Security → Apps and services permissions.
  • To delete everything the app stored, sign out in the app and delete its data folder. Posts already published stay on each platform until you delete them there.
  • For a deletion request or questions, email MartinArturoMuniz@gmail.com. We will respond within 30 days.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly process their data.

Changes

If this policy changes, the effective date above will be updated.
