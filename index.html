Post a video everywhere, once.

socialpost is a personal publishing tool operated by Última Hora con Marty. It uploads the show's own videos to its own accounts on YouTube, Facebook, Instagram and TikTok from a single command.

How it works

  • The owner picks a video on their computer, writes a caption, and chooses which of their accounts to publish to.
  • socialpost signs in to each platform with the platform's official login (OAuth), using permissions the owner grants.
  • The video is uploaded through each platform's official API: the YouTube Data API, Meta Graph API, and TikTok Content Posting API.
  • Posts can go out immediately or at a scheduled time.

Who can use it

socialpost is not a public service. It runs on its owner's computer and is used only to manage the owner's own accounts. There is no sign-up and no user database.

TikTok

With TikTok, socialpost uses Login Kit to connect the owner's TikTok account, and the Content Posting API to publish the owner's videos to that account, showing the account name and the chosen privacy setting before posting.
